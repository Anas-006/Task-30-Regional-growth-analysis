# Task 30 – Regional Growth Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes sales growth across different regions using the Sample Superstore dataset.

The objective is to compare regional sales performance over time, calculate growth rates, and identify regions showing positive or negative growth.

## Objective

- Analyze regional sales performance.
- Compare sales across different years.
- Calculate regional growth percentages.
- Identify high-growth and declining regions.
- Provide business insights based on the analysis.

## Dataset

Dataset Used:
Sample Superstore

Important Columns:
- Order Date
- Region
- Sales
- Year

## Tool Used

Microsoft Excel

## Methodology

1. Imported/opened the Sample Superstore dataset in Excel.
2. Created a Year column using the Order Date.
3. Created a PivotTable with:
   - Region in Rows
   - Year in Columns
   - Sales in Values
4. Compared yearly sales for each region.
5. Calculated year-over-year growth percentage.
6. Created a Regional Sales Growth Trend chart.
7. Reviewed the results and identified key regional trends.

## Growth Rate Formula

Growth Rate (%) =
((Current Year Sales - Previous Year Sales) / Previous Year Sales) × 100

## Regional Growth Results

- Central: 2.42%
- East: 18.57%
- South: -2.58%
- West: -3.85%

## Key Insights

1. East recorded the highest growth at 18.57%, making it the strongest-performing region.

2. Central showed positive growth of 2.42%, indicating a slight improvement in sales.

3. West recorded a decline of 3.85%, making it the weakest-performing region based on the calculated growth rate.

4. South experienced a decline of 2.58%, indicating that its sales performance requires further investigation.

5. Overall regional performance was uneven, with East and Central showing positive growth while South and West experienced declines.

## Business Recommendations

- Investigate the factors contributing to strong growth in the East region.
- Identify successful sales strategies from East and consider applying them to other regions.
- Review product, customer, and sales performance in South and West.
- Monitor declining regions regularly using regional sales reports.
- Develop region-specific strategies instead of applying the same approach to all regions.

## Deliverables

- Excel analysis workbook
- Regional Sales Growth Trend chart
- Growth analysis table
- Key business insights
- PDF report
- Rollback evidence

## Conclusion

The analysis shows that regional sales performance varies significantly. East demonstrated strong positive growth, while South and West showed negative growth. The findings can help management focus on high-performing regions and develop corrective strategies for declining regions.
